<h2 id="verses-new-beginnings">New beginnings</h2>

For the first days of January, when you want to leave last year behind.

> "Remember ye not the former things, neither consider the things of old. Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert."
> **Isaiah 43:18–19** (KJV)

> "Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."
> **2 Corinthians 5:17** (KJV)

> "It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness."
> **Lamentations 3:22–23** (KJV)

> "A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh."
> **Ezekiel 36:26** (KJV)

> "And he that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful."
> **Revelation 21:5** (KJV)

> "Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended: but this one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before, I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus."
> **Philippians 3:13–14** (KJV)

> "Behold, the former things are come to pass, and new things do I declare: before they spring forth I tell you of them."
> **Isaiah 42:9** (KJV)

> "And he hath put a new song in my mouth, even praise unto our God: many shall see it, and fear, and shall trust in the Lord."
> **Psalm 40:3** (KJV)

## How to use a verse through the whole year

A verse helps most when you keep it in front of you after January. A few simple ways:

1. **Pick one.** Read through the list and choose the verse that speaks to where you are right now, not the one that sounds most impressive.
2. **Write it where you'll see it.** On a card in your wallet, on the bathroom mirror, as your phone's lock screen.
3. **Memorize it in the first week.** Say it out loud each morning until you can say it without looking. Our guide on <a href="/blog/how-to-memorize-scripture">how to memorize Scripture</a> has nine simple methods.
4. **Read it in context.** Look up the chapter it comes from, so you know what the verse meant first. Our <a href="/blog/bible-reading-plan-2027">Bible reading plan for 2027</a> will take you through every one of these chapters during the year.
5. **Come back to it monthly.** On the first of each month, write a line about where you've seen the verse come true, or where you need it most. A <a href="/blog/bible-journaling-for-beginners">Bible journal</a> is a good place for that.
