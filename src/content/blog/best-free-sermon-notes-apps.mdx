<p class="article-intro">The best free sermon notes app for most people is the one already on your phone. Apple Notes or Google Keep handle quick typed notes for nothing; YouVersion is free and links your notes to the exact verse; and if you want to record the whole sermon and get an <a href="/blog/best-ai-sermon-transcription-app">automatic transcript</a>, Sermon Keeper is the one app built for it, free to try for 3 days. Below is an honest look at each: what it's genuinely good at, and where "free" comes with a catch.</p>

"Free" covers a lot of ground. Some of these apps are free forever, some are free until you hit a limit, and one is a free trial of a paid app. That matters, so this guide says plainly which is which. No app is listed as free if it quietly isn't. We've ordered the list by the best genuinely-free option first, with the one paid outlier, Sermon Keeper, last, because it's the only one that records and transcribes the whole sermon.

## So which free app should you use?

It comes down to what you actually want to walk away with:

<ul class="article-check">
	<li><strong>Just a few typed points?</strong> Apple Notes or Google Keep: free, instant, already on your phone.</li>
	<li><strong>Notes tied to Scripture for daily study?</strong> YouVersion, free, paired with a method like <a href="/blog/soap-bible-study-method">SOAP</a>.</li>
	<li><strong>A system you fully control?</strong> Notion's free plan, if you enjoy the setup.</li>
	<li><strong>A transcript of audio you already have?</strong> Otter.ai's free minutes, cloud and internet permitting.</li>
	<li><strong>The whole sermon, recorded, timestamped, and transcribed?</strong> Sermon Keeper's free 3-day trial.</li>
</ul>

Most people are best served by pairing two: a free notes app for daily use, and Sermon Keeper for the sermons you actually want to keep. If you're weighing paid options too, our full roundup of the <a href="/blog/best-sermon-notes-apps">best sermon notes apps</a> tests seven of them side by side, and <a href="/blog/how-to-take-sermon-notes">how to take sermon notes</a> walks through the methods themselves.
